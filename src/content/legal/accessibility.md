# Accessibility Statement for PepTalk

Effective date: April 20, 2026

### Accessibility Statement for PepTalk

This is an accessibility statement from The PepTalk Co / Get a PepTalk Limited.

### Measures to support accessibility

The PepTalk Co / Get a PepTalk Limited takes the following measures to ensure accessibility of PepTalk:

* Include accessibility throughout our internal policies.
* We utilise accessibility compliant platforms for the creation of our training courses published on partners and accessibility tools/audits on our websites.

### Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. PepTalk is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard.

### Limitations and alternatives

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility of PepTalk, there may be some limitations. Below is a description of known limitations, and potential solutions. Please contact us if you observe an issue not listed below.

Known limitations for PepTalk:

1. Video content: overlays and graphics are not audio described because they support the content, but aren't essential for the learning experience. We monitor user feedback and keep improving our content. Please get in touch if we can help in any way.
